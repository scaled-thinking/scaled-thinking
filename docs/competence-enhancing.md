# What competence-enhancing means

Most AI tools simply hand you the result. That's handy, but you learn little and need the tool
again next time. Competence-enhancing AI does the work too, but takes you along, with learning
methods from research:

- **It asks before it tells** (Socratic dialogue). A question, then a small hint; the answer comes last.
- **You guess first** (predict, then reveal). You commit to a guess, then see what really happens.
- **It challenges you.** It looks for gaps and weak points in your idea; you decide what to do.
- **You say it in your own words.** Explaining shows what you have really understood.
- **You practise, and it checks later** (spaced repetition). A week later a short test shows what stayed.

**It adapts to you.** It first finds out what you already know, then compares how sure you feel with
how you actually do, and adjusts: more help when you are new, less as you get better. You confirm
every change, and your profile stays on your computer.

Every method has a name, so you can use it again without the AI. In a hurry? Say "just do it".

Competence-enhancing AI makes you more capable, not more dependent: it explains, you decide, and you keep what you learn.
